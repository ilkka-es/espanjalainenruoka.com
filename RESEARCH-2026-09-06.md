# Finnish content research — 6 September 2026

## Scope and limits

Finnish-language searches covered pan con tomate / tomaattileipä, kurttuperunat / ryppyperunat / Kanarian perunat / mojo, crema catalana, and tinto de verano. Existing site content was checked to avoid duplicating the earlier six-post batch. This is a qualitative editorial opportunity assessment, not a keyword-volume report. No publisher analytics, Search Console account, paid keyword dataset or verified share counts were available. Ratings and likes are engagement proxies accumulated over time, not visitor counts or proof of virality. Search result visibility is not a reliable ranking measurement.

## Deliverables

- `/resepti/kurttuperunat-mojo`: four-serving potato recipe plus fresh-pepper mojo, salt explanation and drying guidance.
- `/resepti/crema-catalana`: four-serving stovetop custard, cooling and caramel instructions, troubleshooting.
- `/resepti/pan-con-tomate`: four-serving tomato bread with assembly and ingredient guidance.
- Three original AI-generated editorial images, compressed as 1536 × 1024 JPEGs in `public/images/`.
- Finnish titles and descriptions, ingredient lists, numbered instructions, internal links and the existing generated recipe pages / sitemap integration.

The recipes are independently written editorial formulations, not copied publisher instructions. They have not been kitchen-tested. Each post discloses this and labels its image as an AI-generated serving suggestion. Before presenting these as tested recipes, cook each one and record actual yield, timing and adjustments. Do not add invented ratings, nutrition figures, personal travel anecdotes or “viral” claims.

## How to evaluate traffic after publication

Record the publication date and compare the new pages in Search Console after 28, 56 and 90 days. Review Finnish query impressions, clicks, CTR and average position by page; early data may be sparse. Track tomato bread synonyms together, potato synonyms together, and crema catalana / katalonialainen vanukas together. Improve titles only where impressions and query intent support a change. Link successful articles from relevant existing guides. No traffic increase is guaranteed, and nothing in this batch has been deployed by this task.
